Can a man hide himself in secret places so that I cannot see him? declares the LORD. Do I not fill heaven and earth? declares the LORD.
Jeremiah 23:24 (ESV)

The God Who Sees Everywhere

Title: No Hidden Places: Living Before the Face of God

Reflection:

What does this text teach us? Speaking through Jeremiah, God confronts false prophets who imagined their secret plans and hidden sins were beyond His notice. To them He asks two piercing questions: Can anyone hide from Me? Do I not fill heaven and earth? The implied answer is unmistakable—there is no place, no shadow, no locked room where God’s presence and knowledge do not reach. This is not a threat but a declaration of His nature: God is omnipresent and omniscient, filling all creation with His active, personal awareness.

This truth runs throughout Scripture. Psalm 139 marvels that we cannot flee God’s Spirit or presence, whether we ascend to heaven or descend to the depths. Hebrews 4:13 reminds us that “no creature is hidden from his sight, but all are naked and exposed to the eyes of him to whom we must give account.” God’s omnipresence is not an abstract doctrine; it is the atmosphere in which every thought, word, and deed of our lives takes place.

For the believer, this truth cuts two ways—and both are gracious. First, it exposes us. We cannot compartmentalize our faith, pretending God sees our Sunday worship but not our private thoughts, our public kindness but not our secret bitterness. Total honesty before God is the only honest posture available to us, because pretending otherwise is simply denying reality. Second, this truth comforts us. The same God who sees our sin also sees our suffering, our hidden tears, our unspoken prayers, and our quiet faithfulness that no one else notices. He is not distant or unaware; He is near, filling every corner of our existence with His presence.

This should not produce paralyzing fear but reverent awe. Because of Christ’s finished work, those united to Him by faith stand before this all-seeing God not condemned but justified—clothed in Christ’s righteousness, not our own. Knowing we are fully seen and fully loved in Christ frees us to walk in the light rather than hide in shadows, confessing sin quickly and living with integrity, since pretense before an omnipresent God is both futile and unnecessary.

Confessional Tie-in:

“God comprehendeth all things, and knoweth all things, past, present, and to come… Nothing is to him contingent, or uncertain.” — Westminster Confession of Faith, Chapter II.2

Prayer Prompt:

Lord, You are the God who fills heaven and earth, and there is nowhere I can hide from Your presence. I confess the ways I have tried to compartmentalize my life, acting as though You do not see certain rooms of my heart. Thank You that in Christ, I am fully known and fully loved—help me walk today in the freedom of that truth.

Go Deeper:

Take a few minutes to read Psalm 139 in full, noting how David moves from awe at God’s omniscience to a prayer for God to search his heart. Ask yourself: Is there an area of my life I’ve been treating as “hidden” from God? Bring it into the light today through honest confession, trusting in His steadfast love.

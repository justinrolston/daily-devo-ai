December 25, 2025

For to us a child is born, to us a son is given; and the government shall be upon his shoulder, and his name shall be called Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace.
Isaiah 9:6 (ESV)

The Names That Change Everything

Reflection:

What does this magnificent text teach us? Isaiah 9:6 stands as one of Scripture’s most powerful declarations about the coming Messiah. Written centuries before Christ’s birth, it reveals God’s eternal plan to send His Son as the ultimate answer to humanity’s deepest needs.

Notice the tender beginning: “For to us a child is born, to us a son is given.” The promised Messiah would come not as a conquering warrior, but as a vulnerable infant. Yet this child would bear titles that reveal His divine nature and mission. Each name unveils a facet of who Christ is and what He accomplishes for His people.

As Wonderful Counselor, Jesus provides the wisdom and guidance our hearts desperately need. In a world of conflicting voices and confusing choices, Christ offers perfect counsel through His Word and Spirit. As Mighty God, He possesses the power to accomplish what He promises. Our salvation required divine strength—strength only God Himself could provide.

The title Everlasting Father reveals Christ’s eternal care and protection over His people. Like a faithful father, He nurtures, provides, and guards those entrusted to Him. Finally, as Prince of Peace, Jesus brings the reconciliation between God and sinners that our souls long for. This isn’t merely the absence of conflict, but the positive presence of wholeness and harmony with our Creator.

The phrase “and the government shall be upon his shoulder” reminds us that Christ’s reign extends over all things. Every authority, every power, every circumstance in our lives falls under His sovereign rule. This brings profound comfort when life feels chaotic or uncertain.

For believers today, these names aren’t mere historical titles—they describe our living Savior who actively fulfills these roles in our lives. When anxiety grips us, we have a Wonderful Counselor. When we feel powerless, we serve a Mighty God. When we feel abandoned, we have an Everlasting Father. When conflict surrounds us, we follow the Prince of Peace.

Confessional Tie-in: “The Son of God, the second person of the Trinity, being very and eternal God, of one substance and equal with the Father, did, when the fullness of time had come, take upon Him man’s nature.” — Westminster Confession of Faith 8.2

Prayer Prompt: Lord Jesus, thank You for being everything these glorious names declare. Help me rest in Your perfect counsel, rely on Your mighty power, and trust in Your fatherly care today. Rule as Prince of Peace in my heart and circumstances.

Go Deeper: Take time to meditate on each of Christ’s four names, asking how you specifically need Him to fulfill that role in your life today. Consider memorizing this verse as a source of comfort and strength, letting these truths anchor your heart when circumstances shake your confidence.

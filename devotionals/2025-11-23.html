November 23, 2025

Enter his gates with thanksgiving, and his courts with praise! Give thanks to him; bless his name! For the LORD is good; his steadfast love endures forever, and his faithfulness to all generations.
Psalm 100:4-5 (ESV)

Enter with Thanksgiving

Reflection:

What does this text teach us about approaching God? Psalm 100:4-5 presents a vivid picture of worshipers entering the temple courts, but notice the prescribed attitude: thanksgiving and praise should mark our approach to the Almighty. The psalmist doesn’t merely suggest gratitude as one option among many; rather, he commands it as the proper posture for those who would draw near to God.

This passage reveals something profound about the nature of worship itself. We don’t enter God’s presence to negotiate, to demand, or even primarily to petition—though prayer certainly has its place. Instead, we come with hearts already full of thanksgiving, recognizing what God has already done. This assumes that we serve a God who has acted on our behalf, who has given us reasons to be grateful before we even arrive at His gates.

Why should our hearts overflow with such thanksgiving? The psalmist provides the theological foundation in verse 5: “For the LORD is good; his steadfast love endures forever, and his faithfulness to all generations.” Here we find three unchanging attributes of God that should fuel our gratitude. His goodness means He acts in perfect righteousness and kindness toward His people. His steadfast love—the Hebrew hesed—speaks of His covenant loyalty that doesn’t waver based on our performance. His faithfulness spans generations, meaning what He promised to Abraham, He will fulfill for us.

Consider how this transforms our daily approach to God in prayer and worship. Rather than coming with a catalog of complaints or a desperate bargaining attitude, we can enter His presence with confidence, knowing we approach One who is fundamentally good, unshakably loving, and utterly reliable. This doesn’t mean we avoid bringing our struggles to Him, but it does mean we frame them within the larger reality of His character.

When life feels overwhelming, when circumstances seem to contradict God’s goodness, this psalm calls us to remember what we know to be true about God’s nature. Our feelings may fluctuate, but His steadfast love endures forever. Our circumstances may change, but His faithfulness spans all generations—including ours.

Confessional Tie-in: “God is a Spirit, infinite, eternal, and unchangeable, in his being, wisdom, power, holiness, justice, goodness, and truth.” — Westminster Shorter Catechism, Question 4

Prayer Prompt: Lord, help me to enter Your presence today with a heart full of thanksgiving, recognizing Your unchanging goodness, steadfast love, and faithfulness. When my circumstances tempt me to doubt, remind me of who You are and anchor my soul in these eternal truths.

Go Deeper: Throughout this day, practice entering each moment of prayer—whether brief or extended—with thanksgiving for God’s character before presenting your requests. Reflect on specific ways you’ve experienced His goodness, love, and faithfulness in your own life and family history.

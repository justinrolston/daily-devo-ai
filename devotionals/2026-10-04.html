Do not boast about tomorrow, for you do not know what a day may bring.
Proverbs 27:1 (ESV)

Daily Devotional

Title: Held by a Hand You Cannot See

Reflection:

What does this text teach us? Solomon’s wisdom here cuts directly against one of humanity’s oldest temptations: the illusion of control. To “boast about tomorrow” means to speak and plan as though the future belongs to us, as though our schedules, our health, and our achievements are guaranteed. The Hebrew wisdom tradition consistently pushes back against this presumption, not to make us anxious, but to make us humble and dependent.

This isn’t a prohibition against planning. Scripture elsewhere commends diligence, foresight, and wise preparation. Rather, it’s a warning against the arrogant certainty that we control outcomes we don’t actually control. James picks up this same theme centuries later, rebuking those who say, “Today or tomorrow we will go into such and such a town and spend a year there and trade and make a profit,” without acknowledging, “If the Lord wills, we will live and do this or that” (James 4:13-15).

Beneath this proverb lies a massive theological truth: God alone holds tomorrow. Our lives are not autonomous projects we author and execute. They are gifts, sustained moment by moment by a sovereign God whose purposes stand firm even when ours collapse. This should be deeply humbling—our pride in self-sufficiency is an illusion—but it should also be profoundly comforting. The same God who holds tomorrow beyond our grasp holds it within His own loving hands.

For the believer in Christ, this truth transforms anxiety into trust. We don’t know what a day may bring, but we know who brings it. Jesus taught us to pray for “daily bread,” not a lifetime’s supply—teaching dependence rather than self-sufficiency. Our security was never meant to rest in our ability to predict or control tomorrow, but in the unchanging character of the One who already stands there.

How does this truth confront your planning, your worrying, your confidence in your own strength today? Where have you been quietly boasting about a future only God can guarantee?

Confessional Tie-in:

“God the great Creator of all things doth uphold, direct, dispose, and govern all creatures, actions, and things, from the greatest even to the least, by His most wise and holy providence.” — Westminster Confession of Faith, Chapter V, Article I

Prayer Prompt:

Father, I confess that I often live as though tomorrow belongs to me rather than to You. Thank You for Your patient providence that has sustained me every day of my life until now. Teach me to hold my plans loosely and my trust in You tightly, resting in Your care for whatever this day may bring.

Go Deeper:

Before making plans today, pause and silently acknowledge, “If the Lord wills.” Read James 4:13-17 and consider one area where you’ve been presuming upon tomorrow rather than trusting God with it. What would change in your heart if you truly believed each day was a gift rather than a guarantee?

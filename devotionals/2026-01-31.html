Psalm 86:5 (ESV)

The Lord’s Abundant Character

Psalm 86:5 (ESV) “For you, O Lord, are good and forgiving, abounding in steadfast love to all who call upon you.”

Reflection

What does this text teach us about the character of our God? David presents three essential attributes that form the foundation of our relationship with the Lord: His goodness, His forgiveness, and His steadfast love. These aren’t merely nice thoughts about God—they’re declarations of His unchanging nature that transform how we approach Him in prayer and live as His people.

First, God is good. This goodness isn’t simply moral excellence, but the wellspring of all blessing and benefit that flows from Him. When circumstances overwhelm us or life feels chaotic, we can anchor our souls in this truth: our God is fundamentally, unchangeably good. His plans for us, even when mysterious or difficult, flow from His perfect goodness.

Second, God is forgiving. The Hebrew word here carries the idea of lifting away or pardoning completely. This isn’t grudging forgiveness that holds past sins over our heads, but complete removal of guilt and condemnation. For believers united to Christ, this forgiveness isn’t earned by our repentance—it flows from God’s gracious nature and Christ’s perfect sacrifice. When Satan whispers accusations or our consciences condemn us, we can rest in this truth: our God is a forgiving God.

Third, God abounds in steadfast love. The Hebrew word “chesed” describes God’s covenant faithfulness—His unwavering commitment to His people despite their unfaithfulness. This isn’t love based on our performance, but love rooted in His character and promises. When we feel abandoned or question His care, we remember that His steadfast love never runs dry.

Notice the beautiful condition: these attributes are extended “to all who call upon you.” This isn’t about perfect prayers or eloquent words. It’s about genuine dependence—recognizing our need and turning to Him in faith. Whether we approach God with thanksgiving, confession, desperate pleas, or simple communion, He receives us according to His goodness, forgiveness, and steadfast love.

This truth should revolutionize both our prayer lives and our daily walk. We can approach God’s throne with confidence, not because we’re worthy, but because He is good, forgiving, and abundantly loving to all who call upon Him.

Confessional Tie-in

“This office of mediator the Lord Jesus did most willingly undertake, which that he might discharge, he was made under the law, and did perfectly fulfil it; endured most grievous torments immediately in his soul, and most painful sufferings in his body.” — Westminster Confession of Faith, Chapter VIII, Article 4

Prayer Prompt

Father, thank You that Your character never changes and that You are good, forgiving, and abundant in steadfast love toward me. Help me rest fully in these truths today, especially when circumstances tempt me to doubt Your goodness or when my sins make me want to hide from You.

Go Deeper

Consider how these three attributes of God might change your perspective on a current struggle you’re facing. Spend time in prayer today simply calling upon the Lord, resting in His character rather than focusing on your requests. What would it look like to live with greater confidence in God’s goodness, forgiveness, and steadfast love?

...let it be known to all of you and to all the people of Israel that by the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, whom you crucified, whom God raised from the dead—by him this man is standing before you well. And there is salvation in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved.”
Acts 4:10,12 (ESV)

The Only Name That Saves

Reflection:

What does this text teach us? Peter speaks these bold words to the very religious leaders who orchestrated Jesus’s crucifixion just weeks earlier. Standing before the Sanhedrin, he declares an astounding truth: the man they executed is not only alive but is the exclusive source of salvation for all humanity.

Notice Peter’s unflinching directness. He doesn’t soften the accusation—”whom you crucified”—yet immediately follows with the triumphant reality: “whom God raised from the dead.” This isn’t merely historical information; it’s the heart of the gospel. The crucifixion they meant for evil, God used for our redemption. The resurrection they tried to suppress became the cornerstone of our faith.

The healing of the lame man serves as a living illustration of spiritual salvation. Just as this man could not heal himself and found wholeness only through Christ’s power, so we are spiritually helpless apart from Jesus. No human effort, religious ritual, or moral achievement can bridge the chasm between our sin and God’s holiness.

Peter’s declaration in verse 12 cuts against every notion of religious pluralism. “There is salvation in no one else.” This isn’t narrow-mindedness; it’s divine revelation. Just as there is only one mathematical solution to certain equations, there is only one solution to the problem of sin and death. Jesus alone possesses the credentials: perfect righteousness, substitutionary sacrifice, and resurrection power.

This exclusivity isn’t meant to discourage but to direct us to certainty. In a world of competing truth claims and uncertain promises, we have unshakeable assurance. The name of Jesus—representing all He is and has accomplished—is our guaranteed access to forgiveness, reconciliation with God, and eternal life.

For believers, this truth should fuel both humble gratitude and bold witness. We didn’t find salvation; salvation found us through the only Name that saves. This same Jesus who healed the lame man continues to transform lives today, making the spiritually dead alive and the hopeless whole.

Confessional Tie-in: “This Jesus Christ was appointed by God to be a mediator, the prophet, priest, and king, the head and savior of His church, the heir of all things, and judge of the world. God gave Christ this office from eternity and equipped Him for it by sending Him into the world.” — Belgic Confession, Article 21

Prayer Prompt: Lord Jesus, You alone are the way, the truth, and the life. Thank You that Your name is above every name and that through You alone we have access to the Father. Give us boldness like Peter’s to proclaim this exclusive hope to a world searching for salvation.

Go Deeper: Consider how this truth changes your prayers—you approach God confidently in Jesus’s name, not based on your own worthiness. Reflect on someone in your life who needs to hear that Jesus is the only name by which we must be saved. How might God use you to share this hope?

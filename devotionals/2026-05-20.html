May the God of endurance and encouragement grant you to live in such harmony with one another, in accord with Christ Jesus, that together you may with one voice glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.
Romans 15:5-6 (ESV)

United in Christ’s Glory

Reflection

What does this text teach us? Paul offers a beautiful benediction that reveals God’s heart for His people. He addresses the “God of endurance and encouragement” - qualities desperately needed for Christian unity. This isn’t merely human cooperation Paul envisions, but supernatural harmony that flows from our union with Christ Jesus.

The phrase “in accord with Christ Jesus” is crucial. Our unity isn’t based on shared preferences, personalities, or even theological minutiae, but on our common relationship to Christ. This harmony mirrors the perfect unity within the Trinity itself, where distinct persons exist in perfect accord for the glory of God.

Notice Paul’s ultimate goal: “that together you may with one voice glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.” Christian unity isn’t an end in itself - it serves God’s glory. When believers live in harmony, the world witnesses something supernatural that points beyond human achievement to divine grace.

Yet how challenging this proves in practice! Our fallen nature gravitates toward division, criticism, and self-promotion. We struggle with personalities, preferences, and pride. The Roman church Paul addressed faced tensions between Jewish and Gentile believers - cultural and theological differences that threatened their witness.

This is precisely why Paul appeals to the “God of endurance and encouragement.” Only divine power can produce lasting unity among such diverse, sinful people. God’s endurance with our failures models the patience we need with one another. His encouragement through His Word and Spirit empowers us to persevere when relationships grow difficult.

The “one voice” Paul describes doesn’t mean uniformity in all things, but harmony in the essential - glorifying God together. Like a choir with different parts singing in harmony, the church’s diversity actually enriches our collective praise when unified in Christ.

Consider your own relationships within the body of Christ. Are you contributing to harmony or discord? Do your words encourage endurance in others, or do they tear down? Remember, Christian unity isn’t achieved through human effort alone, but through conscious dependence on the God who transforms hearts and enables what seems impossible - genuine love among very different people, all for His glory.

Confessional Tie-in

“The catholic or universal church, which is invisible, consists of the whole number of the elect, that have been, are, or shall be gathered into one, under Christ the Head thereof; and is the spouse, the body, the fullness of him that fills all in all.” — Westminster Confession of Faith, Chapter 25.1

Prayer Prompt

Father, You are the God of endurance and encouragement. Grant me grace to live in harmony with my brothers and sisters in Christ, putting aside my preferences and pride for the sake of Your glory. Help our church family sing with one voice in praising You, despite our differences.

Go Deeper

Examine your relationships within your local church this week - are you fostering unity or contributing to division through gossip, criticism, or indifference? Consider how you might actively encourage a fellow believer who may be struggling, remembering that God uses us as instruments of His encouragement to others.

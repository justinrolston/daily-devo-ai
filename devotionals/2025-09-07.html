September 07, 2025

Him we proclaim, warning everyone and teaching everyone with all wisdom, that we may present everyone mature in Christ.
Colossians 1:28 (ESV)

The Heart of Christian Ministry

Reflection

“Him we proclaim, warning everyone and teaching everyone with all wisdom, that we may present everyone mature in Christ.” What does this text teach us about the purpose and method of Christian ministry?

Paul reveals the singular focus that should drive all Christian proclamation: Christ himself. Notice the emphasis—”Him we proclaim.” Not merely principles, not self-help wisdom, not even biblical morality divorced from the person of Jesus. The apostle’s ministry centered entirely on declaring who Christ is and what he has accomplished.

This proclamation takes two forms: warning and teaching. The warning speaks to our desperate condition—we are sinners under God’s just wrath, deceived by our hearts, and headed for eternal judgment apart from Christ. The teaching reveals God’s gracious provision—forgiveness, righteousness, and new life found only in Jesus. Both elements require “all wisdom,” suggesting that faithful ministry demands careful study, thoughtful preparation, and dependence on the Holy Spirit to apply truth appropriately to different hearts and circumstances.

But notice Paul’s ultimate goal: “that we may present everyone mature in Christ.” The Greek word for “mature” (teleios) means complete or fully developed. Paul isn’t content with mere conversions; he labors so that every believer would grow into full spiritual maturity. This maturity isn’t measured by human achievement but by conformity to Christ’s character and understanding of his work.

This vision transforms how we think about spiritual growth. Maturity isn’t about accumulating Bible knowledge or achieving moral perfection through willpower. It’s about being so united to Christ that his life increasingly flows through us. It’s about understanding more deeply who we are in him and living out that identity by his Spirit’s power.

Whether you’re a parent, teacher, friend, or simply someone who cares about others’ spiritual welfare, Paul’s example challenges us. Do our conversations point people to Christ? Are we willing to lovingly warn others about sin’s danger while faithfully teaching them about God’s grace? Are we content with surface-level spirituality, or do we long to see genuine transformation in ourselves and others?

The goal remains the same: presenting everyone mature in Christ—not in ourselves, not in religion, but in the person and work of our Savior.

Confessional Tie-in

“The whole counsel of God, concerning all things necessary for his own glory, man’s salvation, faith, and life, is either expressly set down in Scripture, or by good and necessary consequence may be deduced from Scripture.” — Westminster Confession of Faith 1.6

Prayer Prompt

Father, thank you that your Word centers on Christ and leads us to maturity in him. Help me proclaim him faithfully to others, warning them of sin’s danger while teaching them of your grace, that we all might grow into the fullness of who we are in Jesus.

Go Deeper

Consider your conversations this week—do they naturally point others toward Christ and spiritual growth? Reflect on areas where you need to mature in Christ, remembering that this growth comes through his Spirit working in you, not through your own effort.

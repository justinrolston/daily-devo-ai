For God is not unjust so as to overlook your work and the love that you have shown for his name in serving the saints, as you still do.
Hebrews 6:10 (ESV)

God Remembers Your Faithful Service

Reflection

What does this text teach us? The writer of Hebrews offers profound comfort to believers who might question whether their faithful service matters to God. In declaring that “God is not unjust so as to overlook your work and the love that you have shown for his name,” we discover a fundamental truth about our heavenly Father’s character—He sees, He remembers, and He values every act of love performed in His name.

The context is crucial here. The Hebrew Christians were facing persecution and discouragement, likely tempted to abandon their faith. Some may have wondered if their sacrificial service to fellow believers had been worth the cost. The author reminds them that God’s justice includes not only judgment for sin but also faithful recognition of genuine love expressed toward Him.

Notice the specific focus: this isn’t about earning salvation through good works, but about God’s faithful acknowledgment of love demonstrated “for his name.” This love flows from hearts already transformed by grace, expressing itself naturally in “serving the saints.” The phrase “as you still do” indicates ongoing faithfulness despite difficult circumstances.

This truth should encourage every believer who has ever wondered if their quiet acts of service matter. The meals prepared for struggling families, the prayers offered in secret, the patient care for aging parents, the faithful teaching of children—none of this escapes God’s notice. His justice demands that He acknowledge what love has motivated in His name.

Yet we must remember the source of such love. Our capacity to serve others flows from God’s prior love for us in Christ. When we serve fellow believers, we’re not earning God’s favor but expressing the new nature He has given us. The same God who justifies us by faith alone also sanctifies us, producing fruit that honors His name.

This passage also reveals God’s intimate knowledge of His people. He doesn’t merely observe our actions from a distance; He understands the love that motivates our service. He distinguishes between mere duty and genuine affection for His name expressed through care for His people.

For believers walking through seasons of discouragement, wondering if their faithfulness matters, this text offers hope rooted in God’s unchanging character. Our just God will never overlook or forget love shown for His glory.

Confessional Tie-in

“God doth continue to forgive the sins of those that are justified; and although they can never fall from the state of justification, yet they may by their sins fall under God’s fatherly displeasure, and not have the light of his countenance restored unto them, until they humble themselves, confess their sins, beg pardon, and renew their faith and repentance.” — Westminster Confession of Faith, Chapter XI, Article V

Prayer Prompt

Father, thank You that Your justice includes remembering every act of love performed for Your name’s sake. Help me to serve fellow believers not from duty alone but from genuine love for You, trusting that You see and value even my smallest acts of faithfulness.

Go Deeper

Reflect on recent acts of service you’ve performed for other believers. How does knowing that God remembers these acts of love encourage your continued faithfulness? Consider reaching out today to serve a fellow Christian in a practical way, motivated by love for Christ’s name.

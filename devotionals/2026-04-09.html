Hebrews 1:3 (ESV)

The Radiance of Glory

Reflection

What does this magnificent text teach us about Jesus Christ? In just one verse, the author of Hebrews presents us with a breathtaking portrait of our Savior that should leave us both humbled and amazed.

First, we learn that Christ “is the radiance of the glory of God.” This isn’t merely saying that Jesus reflects God’s glory like the moon reflects sunlight. Rather, Christ is the very radiance itself—the outshining of God’s glory. When we look at Jesus, we see God’s character, nature, and perfection displayed in perfect clarity. The invisible God has made himself visible in Christ.

The text continues: Jesus is “the exact imprint of his nature.” The Greek word here refers to the precise impression left by a seal in wax—every detail perfectly reproduced. Jesus isn’t a rough sketch or approximation of God; he is the exact representation of the Father’s essence. To know Jesus is to know God himself.

But notice how this cosmic Christ doesn’t remain distant from our struggles. This same Jesus who “upholds the universe by the word of his power”—maintaining the very fabric of reality through his spoken word—has “made purification for sins.” The One who holds galaxies in place stooped down to cleanse us from our guilt and shame.

This should transform how we view both our Savior and ourselves. When anxiety overwhelms us, we can remember that our Redeemer sustains all things by his powerful word. When shame haunts us, we can recall that this glorious One has purified us completely. When we feel insignificant, we can marvel that the radiance of God’s glory calls us his beloved children.

The phrase “he sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high” signals that Christ’s work of atonement is finished. Unlike Old Testament priests who stood daily offering sacrifices, Jesus sat down because his sacrifice was complete and perfect. From that position of ultimate authority, he continues to uphold us just as surely as he upholds the universe.

This Jesus—cosmic in power yet intimate in love—is worthy of our complete trust, wholehearted worship, and daily dependence.

Confessional Tie-in

“The Son of God, the second person in the Trinity, being very and eternal God, of one substance and equal with the Father, did, when the fullness of time was come, take upon him man’s nature, with all the essential properties, and common infirmities thereof, yet without sin.” — Westminster Confession of Faith 8.2

Prayer Prompt

Lord Jesus, you are the radiance of God’s glory and the exact imprint of his nature. Help me to see you more clearly today and to rest in the truth that the One who upholds the universe by his word has also made complete purification for my sins. Thank you for your finished work on my behalf.

Go Deeper

Today, when you feel overwhelmed by circumstances, remember that your Savior upholds the entire universe—including your situation—by his powerful word. Meditate on this question: How should knowing that Jesus is both the cosmic sustainer and your personal purifier change the way you approach today’s challenges and relationships?

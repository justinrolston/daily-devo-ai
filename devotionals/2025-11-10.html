Daily Devotional

November 10, 2025

God thunders wondrously with his voice; he does great things that we cannot comprehend. For to the snow he says, ‘Fall on the earth,’ likewise to the downpour, his mighty downpour.
Job 37:5-6 (ESV)

The Voice That Commands Creation

Reflection

What does this text teach us? In these verses from Job 37, Elihu marvels at God’s sovereign command over nature itself. The Hebrew word for “thunders” here suggests not just sound, but authoritative declaration—God speaks, and creation responds with immediate obedience. When He says to the snow “Fall on the earth,” there is no delay, no resistance, only perfect compliance.

This passage reveals the breathtaking scope of God’s sovereignty. The same voice that spoke light into existence continues to direct every snowflake’s descent and every raindrop’s path. What appears to us as natural weather patterns are actually the ongoing expressions of God’s active rule over His creation. The snow doesn’t fall by chance, nor does the rain come randomly—both respond to the divine command.

Yet notice what Elihu emphasizes: these are “great things that we cannot comprehend.” Even our most advanced meteorological science only scratches the surface of the intricate systems God governs moment by moment. His ways transcend our understanding, reminding us of our proper place as creatures before our Creator.

For believers walking through seasons of confusion or suffering—as Job certainly was—this truth offers profound comfort. The God who commands every snowfall is the same God orchestrating the circumstances of our lives. Nothing falls outside His sovereign control, not even our deepest trials. Just as snow and rain serve purposes we may not immediately see—nourishing the earth, replenishing water sources, bringing beauty—so too our difficulties often serve divine purposes beyond our comprehension.

This should cultivate both humility and trust in our hearts. Humility, because we’re reminded that God’s thoughts are higher than our thoughts, His ways higher than our ways. Trust, because the One whose voice nature instantly obeys is our heavenly Father who works all things together for the good of those who love Him.

When anxiety rises about circumstances beyond our control, we can remember that the God who governs the weather patterns governs our lives with the same careful attention and perfect wisdom. His voice that commands creation also speaks peace to our troubled hearts.

Confessional Tie-in

“God the great Creator of all things does uphold, direct, dispose, and govern all creatures, actions, and things, from the greatest even to the least, by his most wise and holy providence.” — Westminster Confession of Faith, Chapter 5.1

Prayer Prompt

Father, like the snow and rain that obey Your voice, help me to trust Your sovereign control over every detail of my life. When I cannot comprehend Your ways, grant me faith to rest in Your perfect wisdom and love.

Go Deeper

This week, take time to observe God’s creation around you—whether snow, rain, or sunshine—and let it remind you of His faithful governance over your circumstances. Consider memorizing Isaiah 55:10-11, which beautifully connects God’s control over precipitation with the power of His Word in our lives.

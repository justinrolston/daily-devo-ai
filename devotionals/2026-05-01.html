And without faith it is impossible to please him, for whoever would draw near to God must believe that he exists and that he rewards those who seek him.
Hebrews 11:6 (ESV)

The Essential Foundation of Faith

Hebrews 11:6 (ESV) “And without faith it is impossible to please him, for whoever would draw near to God must believe that he exists and that he rewards those who seek him.”

Reflection

What does this text teach us about our relationship with God? The writer of Hebrews presents faith not as an optional enhancement to spiritual life, but as the absolutely essential foundation. Without faith, pleasing God is impossible—not difficult or unlikely, but categorically impossible.

This verse reveals two fundamental truths that faith must embrace. First, we must believe that God exists. This isn’t merely intellectual acknowledgment that some divine being might be out there, but confident trust in the God who has revealed Himself in Scripture. The God we believe in is not a philosophical abstraction but the living God who created all things, sustains all things, and governs all things according to His perfect will.

Second, faith believes that God “rewards those who seek him.” This doesn’t suggest we earn God’s favor through our seeking, but rather that God graciously responds to those who come to Him. The reward isn’t payment for services rendered, but the generous gift of a Father to His children. The greatest reward is God Himself—His presence, His fellowship, and His glory.

Notice that faith here is active, not passive. It seeks God. True faith doesn’t simply believe facts about God; it pursues relationship with Him. This seeking isn’t anxious striving to win God’s approval, but the natural response of hearts that have been awakened to His beauty and goodness.

For believers walking through seasons of doubt or discouragement, this passage offers both challenge and comfort. Faith doesn’t require the absence of questions, but it does require us to anchor our hearts in what God has revealed about Himself. When circumstances seem to contradict God’s goodness, faith continues to believe that He exists and that He rewards those who seek Him.

The impossibility of pleasing God without faith should drive us not to despair, but to gratitude. If God has given us faith to believe, it is entirely His gift. If we find ourselves seeking Him, it’s because He has first sought us. Our faith, however small or wavering, is precious because it connects us to the infinite God who delights in His children.

Confessional Tie-in

“The principal acts of saving faith are accepting, receiving, and resting upon Christ alone for justification, sanctification, and eternal life, by virtue of the covenant of grace.” — Westminster Confession of Faith, XIV.2

Prayer Prompt

Father, thank You for the gift of faith that enables us to draw near to You. Strengthen our trust in Your existence and Your faithful rewards to those who seek You. Help us to rest confidently in Christ alone, knowing that our faith pleases You not because of its strength, but because of the Savior to whom it clings.

Go Deeper

Consider how your daily choices reflect either faith or unbelief in God’s existence and His faithful rewards. What would change in your life if you truly believed that seeking God is never in vain? Meditate on the specific ways God has already rewarded your seeking—His presence, His Word, His people, and ultimately His Son.

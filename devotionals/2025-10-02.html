The fear of man lays a snare, but whoever trusts in the LORD is safe.
Proverbs 29:25 (ESV)

Freedom from the Fear of Man

Proverbs 29:25 (ESV) “The fear of man lays a snare, but whoever trusts in the LORD is safe.”

Reflection:

What does this text teach us? Solomon presents us with a stark contrast between two ways of living: one enslaved to human opinion and another anchored in divine security. The “fear of man” here isn’t simply being nervous around people—it’s the deep anxiety that comes from making human approval our ultimate concern. This fear becomes a snare, a trap that catches us unaware and holds us captive.

We see this snare everywhere in our daily lives. The student who compromises convictions to fit in. The employee who remains silent about faith to avoid workplace tension. The parent who hesitates to discipline because they want to be liked. The believer who waters down biblical truth to seem more palatable. Each time we prioritize human approval over God’s approval, we step into the snare.

But Scripture offers a liberating alternative: “whoever trusts in the LORD is safe.” The Hebrew word for “safe” suggests being set on high, like a fortress on a hill. When our identity and security rest in God’s unchanging love rather than fickle human opinion, we find true freedom. This isn’t mere positive thinking—it’s grounded in the reality that God has already given us His ultimate approval through Christ.

The gospel transforms our relationship with human opinion. Because Jesus perfectly pleased the Father on our behalf, we no longer need to earn approval through performance. Because He bore our shame, we need not fear human rejection. Because the Creator of the universe calls us His beloved children, what more validation could we possibly need?

This doesn’t mean we become callous toward others or ignore wise counsel. Rather, it means we’re free to love people without needing their approval, to speak truth without fearing their rejection, and to obey God without calculating human costs. When we trust in the Lord’s unshakeable acceptance, we can risk authentic relationships, bold witness, and faithful obedience.

The fear of man promises security but delivers bondage. Trust in the Lord promises safety and actually provides it. Which master will we serve today?

Confessional Tie-in: “The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge, but fools despise wisdom and instruction. We ought to fear God alone, and not man who cannot hurt the soul.” — Heidelberg Catechism, Lord’s Day 33

Prayer Prompt: Father, forgive me for the ways I’ve sought human approval more than Yours. Help me rest securely in Your unchanging love and find my identity in Christ alone. Grant me courage to live faithfully, regardless of human opinion.

Go Deeper: Today, identify one area where fear of man has influenced your choices. Practice trusting God’s approval instead by taking one small step of faithful obedience, regardless of how others might respond. Meditate on passages that reinforce your identity in Christ, such as Romans 8:38-39 or 1 John 3:1.

Thus says the LORD, your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel: “I am the LORD your God, who teaches you to profit, who leads you in the way you should go.
Isaiah 48:17 (ESV)

Our Divine Teacher and Guide

Reflection

What does this text teach us? In Isaiah 48:17, we encounter God speaking with multiple titles that reveal His character and His relationship with His people. He is “the LORD, your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel”—each title pointing to His covenant faithfulness, His saving work, and His absolute purity. But notice what follows: this holy, redeeming God declares that He teaches and leads His people.

The phrase “who teaches you to profit” doesn’t refer to material gain, but to what truly benefits us—what leads to our spiritual flourishing and eternal good. The Hebrew word for “profit” suggests success or advantage, but in God’s economy, true profit means growing in righteousness, wisdom, and fellowship with Him. Our Divine Teacher doesn’t merely give us information; He transforms us through His instruction.

God also declares He “leads you in the way you should go.” This echoes the familiar promise of Psalm 23, where the Good Shepherd guides His sheep. The “way you should go” isn’t always the path we would choose for ourselves. Our natural inclination is to pursue what seems right in our own eyes, but God’s ways are higher than our ways. His leadership often involves redirecting our steps, sometimes through circumstances we wouldn’t welcome.

Here we see both God’s sovereignty and His tender care beautifully intertwined. The same God who rules the universe stoops down to teach and guide each of His children individually. He doesn’t abandon us to figure out life on our own or expect us to navigate spiritual growth through mere willpower.

For believers today, this promise offers profound comfort and practical guidance. When facing difficult decisions, we can trust that our Redeemer-Teacher will provide wisdom through His Word, His Spirit, and His providence. When struggling with sin patterns or spiritual dryness, we remember that the Holy One of Israel is actively teaching us what will truly benefit our souls.

This doesn’t mean the Christian life becomes easy or that God’s guidance always feels clear in the moment. But it does mean we’re never alone in our learning or wandering in our journey. The God who redeemed us continues to shape us, patiently teaching us what leads to genuine flourishing and faithfully guiding us toward our eternal home.

Confessional Tie-in

“The whole counsel of God, concerning all things necessary for his own glory, man’s salvation, faith, and life, is either expressly set down in scripture, or by good and necessary consequence may be deduced from scripture.” — Westminster Confession of Faith, Chapter 1, Article 6

Prayer Prompt

Lord, thank You for being both my Redeemer and my Teacher. Help me to trust Your instruction even when it challenges my preferences, and to follow Your leading even when the path seems unclear. Make me teachable and willing to walk in Your ways.

Go Deeper

Consider a current decision or struggle you’re facing—how might God be teaching you “to profit” through this situation? Spend time in His Word today, asking the Spirit to show you the way you should go, trusting that your faithful Teacher-Guide will provide the wisdom you need.

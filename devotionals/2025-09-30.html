And it is my prayer that your love may abound more and more, with knowledge and all discernment, so that you may approve what is excellent, and so be pure and blameless for the day of Christ...
Philippians 1:9-10 (ESV)

Love That Grows in Wisdom

Reflection

What does this text teach us? Paul’s prayer for the Philippians reveals a profound understanding of spiritual maturity. He doesn’t simply pray for their love to increase—he prays for their love to “abound more and more, with knowledge and all discernment.” This is love that grows not just in intensity, but in wisdom and understanding.

The apostle recognizes that love without discernment can become sentimental and misguided. We live in a world that often equates love with accepting everything and everyone without distinction. But Paul’s prayer shows us that true Christian love is informed love—love guided by knowledge of God’s truth and discernment of His will. This knowledge isn’t merely intellectual; it’s the deep, experiential knowledge that comes from walking with Christ.

The purpose of this growing, discerning love is intensely practical: “so that you may approve what is excellent.” The Greek word for “approve” suggests the testing of metals to determine their purity. When our love is shaped by biblical knowledge and spiritual discernment, we develop the ability to distinguish between what is merely good and what is truly excellent—what honors God most.

This discerning love leads to purity and blamelessness “for the day of Christ.” Paul isn’t suggesting we can achieve sinless perfection, but rather that God’s work in us produces genuine holiness. Our justification before God is complete in Christ, but our sanctification—our growing in actual righteousness—continues throughout our lives.

Notice the connection between love, wisdom, and holy living. It’s not enough to have warm feelings toward God and others; our affections must be educated by Scripture and refined by the Holy Spirit. When they are, we begin to love what God loves and to choose what truly pleases Him.

This prayer challenges us to examine our own spiritual growth. Are we growing not just in religious activity, but in discerning love? Are we learning to distinguish between cultural Christianity and biblical faithfulness? God’s goal for us isn’t merely that we avoid obvious sins, but that we actively choose what is most excellent for His glory.

Confessional Tie-in

“The whole counsel of God, concerning all things necessary for his own glory, man’s salvation, faith, and life, is either expressly set down in Scripture, or by good and necessary consequence may be deduced from Scripture.” — Westminster Confession of Faith 1.6

Prayer Prompt

Father, grant me a love that grows not only in warmth but in wisdom. Give me discernment to approve what is excellent in Your sight, and shape my heart to choose what brings You the greatest glory, preparing me for the day of Christ.

Go Deeper

Examine your recent decisions through the lens of “approving what is excellent.” Were your choices based on convenience, cultural acceptance, or biblical discernment? Ask God to show you areas where your love needs to grow in knowledge, and spend time in Scripture allowing His truth to shape your affections.

June 04, 2026

Yours, O LORD, is the greatness and the power and the glory and the victory and the majesty, for all that is in the heavens and in the earth is yours. Yours is the kingdom, O LORD, and you are exalted as head above all.
1 Chronicles 29:11 (ESV)

The Kingdom That Cannot Be Shaken

Reflection:

What does this magnificent text teach us? David’s words ring out like a triumphant anthem, declaring truths that should shape how we view God, ourselves, and our circumstances. This isn’t merely poetic language—it’s a theological declaration that cuts to the heart of reality itself.

Notice how David attributes everything to the Lord: greatness, power, glory, victory, and majesty. These aren’t qualities God possesses alongside other beings who might share them; they belong to Him exclusively. The Hebrew word for “greatness” suggests incomparable magnitude, while “power” speaks of His absolute ability to accomplish His will. When we grasp this, our perspective on daily struggles begins to shift.

David’s reasoning is crucial: God owns everything “in the heavens and in the earth.” This comprehensive ownership isn’t merely legal—it’s foundational to reality itself. God doesn’t rule by conquest or appointment; He rules because all creation flows from His sovereign will. The kingdom is His by right, not by might.

What does this mean for believers today? When anxiety threatens to overwhelm us, we remember that our circumstances fall under the authority of One whose power is absolute. When success tempts us toward pride, we recall that even our abilities and opportunities are His gifts. When injustice seems to prevail, we trust that the King “exalted as head above all” will ultimately vindicate His righteousness.

This truth should cultivate both humility and confidence in our hearts. Humility, because we recognize our complete dependence on God’s grace in every area of life. We bring nothing to the table that didn’t originate with Him. Yet we can also walk with quiet confidence, knowing that our lives are held securely in hands that wield all power and authority.

David’s words also remind us that our earthly struggles, while real and often painful, are temporary. Kings rise and fall, economies fluctuate, relationships disappoint—but God’s kingdom remains unshakable. This doesn’t minimize our present difficulties, but it does provide the eternal perspective that enables us to endure them faithfully.

How then shall we live? With grateful recognition that every breath, every blessing, every opportunity for service flows from His gracious hand. Our response should be worship that acknowledges His supreme worth and service that reflects His character to a watching world.

Confessional Tie-in:

“God the great Creator of all things does uphold, direct, dispose, and govern all creatures, actions, and things, from the greatest even to the least, by his most wise and holy providence.” — Westminster Confession of Faith 5.1

Prayer Prompt:

Lord, help me live today with the deep awareness that You are sovereign over every detail of my life. When I’m tempted to worry or boast, remind me that Your kingdom is unshakable and Your purposes will prevail.

Go Deeper:

Consider specific areas where you struggle to trust God’s sovereignty—perhaps finances, relationships, or health. How might remembering His absolute authority over “all that is in the heavens and in the earth” change your perspective? Spend time meditating on other passages that celebrate God’s kingship, such as Psalm 93 or Daniel 4:34-35.

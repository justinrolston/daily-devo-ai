[I Love the LORD] I love the LORD, because he has heard my voice and my pleas for mercy. Because he inclined his ear to me, therefore I will call on him as long as I live.
Psalm 116:1-2 (ESV)

When God Listens: A Love Born from Answered Prayer

Reflection

What does this text teach us? The psalmist declares a profound truth: his love for the LORD flows directly from experiencing God’s attentive care. “I love the LORD, because he has heard my voice and my pleas for mercy.” This isn’t abstract theology—it’s personal testimony born from real encounter with the living God.

Notice the intimate language: God “inclined his ear” to the psalmist. This beautiful Hebrew expression pictures the Almighty bending down, leaning in close to catch every word of our prayers. The sovereign Creator of the universe doesn’t strain to hear us because He lacks power, but because He chooses to give us His full, tender attention. This is the God who numbers our tears and knows our thoughts before we speak them.

The psalmist’s response reveals something crucial about how love grows in the believer’s heart. We don’t manufacture love for God through willpower or religious duty. Rather, love springs naturally from experiencing His grace. When we truly grasp that the holy God hears our desperate pleas, receives our broken confessions, and responds with mercy rather than judgment, how can our hearts not be stirred to love?

This creates a beautiful cycle of grace. Because God has proven faithful in hearing our prayers, “therefore I will call on him as long as I live.” Past mercies fuel future confidence. Yesterday’s answered prayers become today’s encouragement to pray again. This isn’t presumption—it’s the proper response of a heart that has learned to trust God’s character.

Yet we must remember that God’s “hearing” doesn’t always mean immediate relief from our circumstances. Sometimes His answer is strength to endure, wisdom to understand, or simply His presence in our suffering. The psalmist celebrates not just getting what he wanted, but discovering a God who truly cares enough to listen.

For believers today, this passage calls us to examine our prayer lives and our motivations for loving God. Do we love Him primarily for what He gives us, or because of who He is? The psalmist finds the perfect balance—loving God genuinely while honestly acknowledging that this love grew through experiencing God’s faithful responses to prayer.

Confessional Tie-in

“Prayer is an offering up of our desires unto God, for things agreeable to his will, in the name of Christ, with confession of our sins, and thankful acknowledgment of his mercies.” — Westminster Shorter Catechism, Question 98

Prayer Prompt

Heavenly Father, thank You for inclining Your ear to hear my prayers, not because I deserve it but because of Your great mercy. Help me to love You more deeply as I experience Your faithful responses to my cries. Give me confidence to call upon You all the days of my life.

Go Deeper

Reflect on specific times when God has clearly heard and answered your prayers—how did those experiences deepen your love for Him? Consider keeping a prayer journal to record both your requests and God’s responses, creating your own testimony of His faithfulness. This week, let past mercies fuel fresh confidence in approaching God with your current needs.

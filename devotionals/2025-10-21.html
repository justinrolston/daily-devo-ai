I delight to do your will, O my God; your law is within my heart.”
Psalm 40:8 (ESV)

Delight in Divine Will

Psalm 40:8 (ESV): “I delight to do your will, O my God; your law is within my heart.”

Reflection

What does this text teach us? David speaks here not merely of dutiful obedience, but of joyful delight in God’s will. The Hebrew word for “delight” conveys pleasure and satisfaction—the same word used to describe God’s delight in His people. This isn’t grudging compliance but wholehearted embrace of what God desires.

Notice the progression: delight leads to action (“to do your will”), which flows from an internal reality (“your law is within my heart”). True obedience springs from a transformed heart where God’s Word has taken root. This isn’t external conformity to rules but internal alignment with God’s character and purposes.

The phrase “your law is within my heart” points to something deeper than mere knowledge of Scripture. It speaks of God’s Word becoming part of our very nature, shaping our desires and directing our steps. When Scripture penetrates our hearts, we begin to want what God wants. His will becomes our delight rather than our burden.

Yet we must be honest about our experience. How often do we find God’s will delightful? Too frequently, we view His commands as restrictive rather than liberating, as burdens rather than blessings. This reveals our need for the Spirit’s ongoing work of sanctification. The same God who gave David this heart of delight continues His transforming work in us.

This psalm ultimately points beyond David to Christ, who perfectly embodied this reality. Jesus said, “My food is to do the will of him who sent me” (John 4:34). Where we fail to delight consistently in God’s will, Christ succeeded completely. His perfect obedience becomes ours through faith, and His Spirit works within us to increasingly align our hearts with the Father’s will.

The Christian life isn’t about forcing ourselves to obey a reluctant heart, but about having our hearts progressively changed so that we genuinely desire what God desires. As we meditate on His Word and walk in His Spirit, we discover that His will isn’t contrary to our joy but the very source of it. God’s commands aren’t arbitrary restrictions but loving guidance from our Creator who knows what brings true flourishing.

Confessional Tie-in

“The whole counsel of God, concerning all things necessary for his own glory, man’s salvation, faith, and life, is either expressly set down in Scripture, or by good and necessary consequence may be deduced from Scripture: unto which nothing at any time is to be added, whether by new revelations of the Spirit, or traditions of men.” — Westminster Confession of Faith 1.6

Prayer Prompt

Father, thank you that your will is not burdensome but good, pleasing, and perfect. Transform my heart so that I increasingly delight in what you desire. Help me find joy in obedience and satisfaction in following your ways.

Go Deeper

Consider one area where you struggle to see God’s will as delightful. Ask the Spirit to show you how His command in that area actually serves your good and His glory. Spend time this week memorizing Scripture, asking God to write His Word more deeply on your heart.

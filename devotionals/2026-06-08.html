GOD, the Lord, is my strength; he makes my feet like the deer's; he makes me tread on my high places. To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments.
Habakkuk 3:19 (ESV)

Finding Strength for Life’s Steep Climbs

Reflection

What does this magnificent conclusion to Habakkuk’s prayer teach us about the nature of true strength? The prophet has wrestled with God over injustice and suffering, and now he arrives at a stunning declaration of trust. “GOD, the Lord, is my strength” - not a source of strength among many, but the strength that sustains him.

The imagery Habakkuk employs is vivid and purposeful. Deer are renowned for their ability to navigate treacherous mountain terrain with grace and confidence. Their hooves are perfectly designed for gripping rocky surfaces, allowing them to bound up steep inclines and traverse narrow ledges that would be impossible for other creatures. When the prophet declares that God makes his feet like the deer’s, he’s describing a supernatural transformation of his capacity to handle life’s most challenging circumstances.

The “high places” in ancient Near Eastern culture were often associated with both worship and danger - elevated locations where one drew near to God but also faced exposure and risk. Habakkuk is saying that God doesn’t merely help him survive difficult seasons; He enables him to thrive in them, to “tread” with confidence where others might stumble and fall.

This strength isn’t self-generated or mustered through positive thinking. It flows from the character of “GOD, the Lord” - the covenant-keeping God who has bound Himself to His people. The Hebrew uses both Yahweh (the covenant name) and Adonai (sovereign Lord), emphasizing both God’s faithfulness and His absolute authority.

Notice that this transformation affects how we move through our circumstances, not necessarily the circumstances themselves. The mountains remain steep, the paths narrow, but our footing becomes sure. God doesn’t always remove our challenges, but He transforms our capacity to navigate them with grace and confidence.

For believers today, this means that seasons of uncertainty, financial pressure, relational conflict, or spiritual dryness need not defeat us. The same God who steadied Habakkuk’s feet offers us His strength. Through union with Christ, we have access to divine resources that enable us to not merely endure but to advance with purpose, even when the path seems impossible.

The concluding musical notation reminds us that this isn’t private comfort but a song meant to be shared - truth worth proclaiming to encourage others climbing their own steep places.

Confessional Tie-in

“They who are effectually called do in this life partake of justification, adoption, sanctification, and the several benefits which in this life do either accompany or flow from them.” — Westminster Confession of Faith 10.1

Prayer Prompt

Lord, You are my strength when my own resources fail. Transform my anxious heart and unsteady steps. Grant me the sure footing of faith to navigate today’s challenges with grace, knowing You make me able to tread confidently in places where I could never walk alone.

Go Deeper

Identify one “high place” in your life where you need God’s strength today - perhaps a difficult relationship, work challenge, or area of spiritual growth. Spend time meditating on God’s faithfulness in past difficulties. How might He be preparing you for greater trust and service through current struggles?

And this is the confidence that we have toward him, that if we ask anything according to his will he hears us. And if we know that he hears us in whatever we ask, we know that we have the requests that we have asked of him.
1 John 5:14-15 (ESV)

Praying with Confidence

Reflection

What does this text teach us about prayer? John reveals a remarkable truth: believers can approach God with genuine confidence. But this isn’t presumptuous boldness based on our own merit—it’s grounded in something far more reliable: God’s will and character.

The key phrase here is “according to his will.” This isn’t a limiting condition that restricts our prayers, but rather the very foundation that makes confident prayer possible. When we pray according to God’s will, we’re not trying to bend His arm or change His mind. Instead, we’re aligning ourselves with His perfect purposes and expressing trust in His wisdom.

But how do we know God’s will? Scripture reveals His heart—He desires our sanctification, the spread of the gospel, care for the suffering, and ultimately His own glory. When we pray for forgiveness, spiritual growth, opportunities to serve, or wisdom in difficult circumstances, we’re praying squarely within His revealed will.

Notice the beautiful progression John describes: we ask according to God’s will, He hears us, and we receive what we’ve requested. This isn’t a formula for getting what we want, but a description of how prayer works when our hearts are aligned with God’s purposes. The confidence comes not from the strength of our faith or the eloquence of our words, but from knowing that our heavenly Father delights to give good gifts to His children.

This passage transforms how we understand unanswered prayer. Sometimes what feels like God’s silence is actually His loving redirection. When we pray for something outside His will, His “no” is itself an answer—protecting us from what would ultimately harm us or hinder His greater purposes.

The confidence John describes should encourage believers who sometimes feel their prayers bounce off the ceiling. God hears every prayer offered in faith, even our stumbling, imperfect requests. He knows our hearts and translates our groaning into prayers that align with His will.

This confidence also calls us to persistent prayer. Knowing that God delights in our requests according to His will should drive us to our knees more often, not less. We can pour out our hearts freely, trusting that our Father will grant what’s best and redirect what isn’t.

Confessional Tie-in

“We pray unto God only in the name of Christ, by the help of His Spirit, according to His will, with understanding, reverence, humility, fervency, faith, love and perseverance.” — Westminster Shorter Catechism, Answer 99

Prayer Prompt

Father, thank You that we can come boldly before Your throne of grace through Christ. Help us to pray according to Your will, trusting that You hear and answer every prayer offered in faith. Grant us wisdom to discern Your purposes and patience to wait for Your perfect timing.

Go Deeper

Examine your recent prayers—are they aligned with God’s revealed will in Scripture? Consider keeping a prayer journal to track how God answers over time. Meditate on other passages about prayer like Matthew 6:9-13 and Romans 8:26-27 to deepen your understanding of this privilege.

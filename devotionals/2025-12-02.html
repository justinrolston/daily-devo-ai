December 02, 2025

[The Supremacy of God's Son] Long ago, at many times and in many ways, God spoke to our fathers by the prophets, but in these last days he has spoken to us by his Son, whom he appointed the heir of all things, through whom also he created the world.
Hebrews 1:1-2 (ESV)

God’s Final and Perfect Word

Reflection:

What does this magnificent opening to Hebrews teach us? The author begins with a sweeping declaration about God’s communication throughout history. For centuries, God spoke through prophets—men like Moses, Isaiah, and Jeremiah—delivering His word “at many times and in many ways.” These revelations came through dreams, visions, direct encounters, and written scrolls. Each prophet carried a piece of God’s unfolding plan, like individual notes in a grand symphony.

But then came the crescendo: “in these last days he has spoken to us by his Son.” This isn’t merely another prophetic voice added to the chorus. This is God’s final, complete, and perfect Word. Jesus Christ is not just a messenger; He is the Message itself. While prophets delivered God’s word, Jesus is the Word made flesh.

Notice how the text exalts Christ’s supremacy. He is “heir of all things”—everything belongs to Him by divine right. Even creation itself came “through” Him, revealing that the Son who would later take on human flesh was the eternal Word through whom all things were made. The carpenter from Nazareth is the cosmic Creator.

This truth transforms how we read Scripture and approach God. We no longer wonder if God has more to say or if we’re missing some secret revelation. Christ is God’s complete and final word to humanity. In Him, all the prophetic fragments find their fulfillment and meaning.

For believers facing uncertainty, this brings profound peace. God has not left us guessing about His character, His will, or His love. We have His perfect revelation in Jesus Christ. When we want to know what God is like, we look to Christ. When we need wisdom for daily decisions, we turn to the One who embodies divine wisdom.

The prophets pointed forward to Christ; we look back to Him with full clarity. We possess what they longed to see—God’s complete revelation in His beloved Son.

Confessional Tie-in: “It pleased the Lord, at sundry times, and in diverse manners, to reveal himself, and to declare that his will unto his church; and afterwards for the better preserving and propagating of the truth… to commit the same wholly unto writing; which makes the Holy Scripture to be most necessary.” — Westminster Confession of Faith, I.1

Prayer Prompt: Father, thank You for speaking clearly through Your Son. Help me treasure Christ as Your perfect Word and find in Him all I need for life and godliness. May I never seek revelation beyond what You have graciously given in Scripture.

Go Deeper: How does knowing Christ as God’s final word affect your approach to decision-making and spiritual growth? Consider studying other passages that highlight Christ’s supremacy, such as Colossians 1:15-20, to deepen your wonder at His magnificent person and work.

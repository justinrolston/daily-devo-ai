To them God chose to make known how great among the Gentiles are the riches of the glory of this mystery, which is Christ in you, the hope of glory. Him we proclaim, warning everyone and teaching everyone with all wisdom, that we may present everyone mature in Christ.
Colossians 1:27-28 (ESV)

The Treasure Within: Christ as Our Hope of Glory

Reflection:

What does this remarkable passage teach us about the Christian life? Paul reveals one of the most stunning truths in all of Scripture: “Christ in you, the hope of glory.” This isn’t merely poetic language—it’s the bedrock reality of our faith.

The apostle speaks of a “mystery” that God chose to reveal. In biblical terms, a mystery isn’t something impossible to understand, but rather a truth once hidden that God has now made known. For centuries, God’s people wondered how the nations would be brought into His covenant family. The answer defied all expectations: Christ Himself would dwell within believers, both Jew and Gentile alike.

Consider the magnitude of this truth. The same Christ who spoke worlds into existence, who walked on water and conquered death, has taken up residence in your heart through faith. This isn’t a temporary visit or conditional arrangement—He has made you His temple. When Paul calls this “the hope of glory,” he means that Christ’s presence within us is both the guarantee and the beginning of our future glorification with Him.

But notice how Paul immediately connects this glorious truth to practical ministry. Because we possess this treasure, we have work to do. The Gospel must be proclaimed, believers must be warned against error and taught in wisdom, all with one goal: “that we may present everyone mature in Christ.”

This reveals God’s purpose in our salvation. He doesn’t save us merely to escape judgment, though that would be mercy enough. He saves us to transform us, to bring us to spiritual maturity that reflects His character. This maturation isn’t our work—it’s His work through us, empowered by the very Christ who lives within.

The Christian life, then, is both a possession and a process. We possess Christ fully from the moment of faith, yet we’re called to grow into the fullness of what that means. Every struggle with sin, every step of obedience, every act of service flows from this central reality: Christ lives in us, and He is transforming us into His likeness for His glory.

Confessional Tie-in:

“The principal acts of saving faith are accepting, receiving, and resting upon Christ alone for justification, sanctification, and eternal life, by virtue of the covenant of grace.” — Westminster Confession of Faith 14.2

Prayer Prompt:

Father, I marvel that You have chosen to dwell within me through Your Son. Help me to live today in the reality of Christ’s presence, drawing on His strength for every challenge and growing in the maturity You desire for me.

Go Deeper:

Take time this week to meditate on what it means that Christ actually dwells within you—not as a distant helper, but as your very life. Consider how this truth should shape your responses to both temptation and opportunity, knowing that the One who conquered death is your constant companion.

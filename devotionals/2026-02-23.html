Do they not go astray who devise evil? Those who devise good meet steadfast love and faithfulness.
Proverbs 14:22 (ESV)

The Path of Wisdom: Where Our Plans Lead

Reflection

“Do they not go astray who devise evil? Those who devise good meet steadfast love and faithfulness.” This proverb presents us with a fundamental truth about human nature and God’s moral order: the direction of our heart inevitably determines the destination of our life.

What does this text teach us? Solomon draws a stark contrast between two types of people—those who devise evil and those who devise good. The Hebrew word for “devise” suggests careful planning and intentional scheming. This isn’t about accidental sin or momentary lapses in judgment, but about the deliberate orientation of our hearts and minds.

Those who devise evil “go astray”—they wander from the path of life like sheep without a shepherd. There’s an inherent futility in wickedness; sin promises much but delivers only emptiness and separation from God. Evil plans may seem clever in the moment, but they lead inevitably to spiritual bankruptcy.

In beautiful contrast, those who devise good “meet steadfast love and faithfulness.” The word “meet” suggests an encounter—as if steadfast love and faithfulness are waiting along the path to greet the righteous. This isn’t merely human approval, but God’s covenant love (hesed) and His unwavering faithfulness meeting those who align their hearts with His will.

But here we must pause and examine ourselves honestly. Apart from God’s grace, which of us naturally devises good? Our hearts are prone to wander, prone to devise schemes that serve self rather than God. The gospel reminds us that we don’t naturally choose the path of righteousness—God must first change our hearts.

Yet for believers in Christ, this proverb becomes both promise and calling. Having been justified by faith alone, we’re now being sanctified by the Spirit who enables us to devise good rather than evil. Our plans, our dreams, our daily choices can increasingly reflect God’s character as we grow in grace.

Consider your own planning and scheming today. What occupies your thoughts? What do you spend time devising? The promise stands: when our hearts are aligned with God’s purposes, we don’t simply do good—we encounter His steadfast love and faithfulness in return. This is the beautiful economy of God’s kingdom, where righteousness brings its own reward in the form of deeper fellowship with our faithful Creator.

Confessional Tie-in

“The chief end of man is to glorify God, and to enjoy him forever.” — Westminster Shorter Catechism, Question 1

Prayer Prompt

Heavenly Father, examine my heart and reveal the true nature of my plans and desires. Help me devise good that glorifies You rather than schemes that serve only myself. Thank You for Your steadfast love that meets me on the path of righteousness through Christ.

Go Deeper

Before making any significant decision today, ask yourself: “Does this plan align with God’s character and purposes?” Consider keeping a brief journal this week of how you see God’s faithfulness meeting you as you seek to honor Him in your choices and planning.

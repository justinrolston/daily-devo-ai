On God rests my salvation and my glory; my mighty rock, my refuge is God.
Psalm 62:7 (ESV)

Our Unshakeable Foundation

Reflection

What does this text teach us? In Psalm 62:7, David makes four profound declarations about God’s role in his life, each building upon the other like stones in a fortress wall. “On God rests my salvation and my glory; my mighty rock, my refuge is God.” These aren’t mere poetic flourishes—they’re theological anchors that reveal where true security is found.

First, David declares that his salvation rests on God. The Hebrew word for “rests” suggests something settled, established, and unmovable. David isn’t saying his salvation depends partly on God and partly on his own efforts. Rather, his deliverance—both temporal and eternal—finds its complete foundation in God alone. This echoes throughout Scripture: salvation belongs to the Lord, not to human strength, wisdom, or righteousness.

Second, his glory rests on God. In biblical terms, glory often refers to one’s honor, reputation, or worth. David recognizes that any dignity or significance he possesses flows from his relationship with the Almighty. Apart from God, we have no true glory—only the fleeting praise of men or the false glory of self-righteousness.

The imagery then shifts to geological metaphors that would resonate deeply with anyone familiar with the rocky terrain of ancient Israel. God is David’s “mighty rock”—not just stable, but powerfully so. Rocks don’t merely endure; they provide foundation for everything built upon them. When storms rage and foundations are shaken, what matters most is what lies beneath.

Finally, God is his refuge. This speaks to accessibility and safety. A rock might be strong but unreachable; a refuge is both secure and welcoming. In times of danger, distress, or doubt, believers can flee to God as their safe haven.

These truths speak directly to our daily struggles with insecurity, anxiety, and the pressure to establish our own worth. We live in a culture that demands we build our identity on career success, relationships, achievements, or possessions. But David models a different way—finding his ultimate security, significance, and safety in the unchanging character of God.

When circumstances shift, when people disappoint us, when our own strength fails, we can echo David’s confidence: our salvation, our glory, our very foundation rests securely on the One who never changes, never fails, and never abandons His people.

Confessional Tie-in

“It is impossible for those who are once truly grafted into Christ by true faith to fall totally or finally away from that faith. This perseverance of the saints does not depend on their own free will but on the unchangeable decree of election flowing from God’s eternal and unchangeable love.” — Belgic Confession, Article 24

Prayer Prompt

Father, I confess that I often seek security in temporary things and find my worth in worldly achievements. Help me rest completely in You as my salvation, glory, mighty rock, and refuge. When anxiety rises and circumstances shake, remind me that my foundation is unshakeable because it rests in You alone.

Go Deeper

Consider what you typically turn to first when facing stress or insecurity. How might consciously viewing God as your “mighty rock” change your response to current challenges? Memorize this verse and recall it the next time you’re tempted to find your security or worth in something other than God’s unchanging character and promises.

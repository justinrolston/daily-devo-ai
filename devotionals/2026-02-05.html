The Earth is Full of His Love

Psalm 33:4-5 - “For the word of the LORD is upright, and all his work is done in faithfulness. He loves righteousness and justice; the earth is full of the steadfast love of the LORD.”

Reflection

What does this text teach us about the character of our God? The psalmist declares four foundational truths that should anchor our souls: God’s word is upright, His work is faithful, He loves what is right and just, and His steadfast love fills the earth.

First, God’s word is “upright” - perfectly straight, without deviation or deceit. In a world where truth seems relative and words often mislead, we can trust completely in what God has spoken. His promises are reliable because He Himself is reliable. When Scripture says you are forgiven in Christ, when it promises His presence in trials, when it assures you of eternal life - these are not mere hopes but unshakeable realities.

Second, “all his work is done in faithfulness.” From the grand sweep of redemptive history to the intimate details of your daily life, God operates with perfect consistency to His character and promises. The same faithfulness that delivered Israel from Egypt and raised Christ from the dead is at work in your circumstances today, even when you cannot see it.

Third, God “loves righteousness and justice.” This isn’t merely tolerance or preference - it’s deep, passionate love for what is right. In our broken world where injustice seems to prevail, we can trust that our God sees, cares, and will ultimately make all things right. His love for justice should also shape our own hearts, moving us to pursue what is right in our relationships, work, and community.

Finally, “the earth is full of the steadfast love of the LORD.” The Hebrew word hesed speaks of covenant love - loyal, persistent, unbreakable. This isn’t sentiment but commitment. Everywhere you look, from the sunrise that greets you each morning to the breath in your lungs, you are surrounded by expressions of God’s covenant faithfulness to His people.

When anxiety whispers that God has forgotten you, when circumstances suggest He doesn’t care, when the future feels uncertain - remember this psalm. You live in a world saturated with the steadfast love of One whose word never fails and whose works are always faithful.

Confessional Tie-in

“The Word of God, which is contained in the Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments, is the only rule to direct us how we may glorify and enjoy him.” — Westminster Shorter Catechism, Question 2

Prayer Prompt

Father, thank You that Your word stands firm and Your faithfulness endures forever. Help me to trust in Your steadfast love today, especially in those areas where I’m tempted to doubt Your goodness. Open my eyes to see evidence of Your covenant love surrounding me.

Go Deeper

Look for three specific evidences of God’s steadfast love in your life today - perhaps in provision, relationships, or unexpected grace. Consider memorizing this passage to anchor your heart when circumstances tempt you to question God’s faithfulness.

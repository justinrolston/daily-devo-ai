In the Fullness of Time

Galatians 4:4-5 (ESV) “But when the fullness of time had come, God sent forth his Son, born of woman, born under the law, to redeem those who were under the law, so that we might receive adoption as sons.”

Reflection:

What does this magnificent text teach us about God’s perfect timing and gracious plan? Paul reveals that Christ’s coming was not random or hurried, but occurred at precisely the right moment—”when the fullness of time had come.” This phrase speaks to God’s sovereign orchestration of history. The Roman roads were built, Greek was widely spoken, Jewish expectation was heightened, and the world was prepared for the gospel’s spread. Our God is never early, never late, but always perfectly on time.

Notice the profound mystery Paul unveils: God “sent forth his Son.” This sending implies both Christ’s pre-existence and the Father’s initiative in salvation. The eternal Son willingly entered human history, becoming what He had never been—man—while remaining what He always was—God. He was “born of woman,” fully human, experiencing the same frailties we face yet without sin. He was “born under the law,” placing Himself under the very commandments that condemn us.

But why? Paul gives us the glorious double purpose: redemption and adoption. Christ came “to redeem those who were under the law.” Like slaves in a marketplace, we stood condemned, unable to free ourselves from sin’s bondage. But Christ paid the price—His own precious blood—to purchase our freedom. The law that once held us captive now declares us righteous through Christ’s perfect obedience.

Yet redemption was not the end goal but the means to something even more wonderful: “so that we might receive adoption as sons.” We are not merely freed slaves but beloved children, brought into God’s family with all the privileges that relationship entails. We bear His name, inherit His promises, and enjoy intimate access to His presence.

This truth transforms how we face each day. When circumstances feel chaotic, remember that your Heavenly Father orchestrates all things in perfect timing. When sin weighs heavy, remember that Christ has redeemed you completely. When you feel distant from God, remember that you are His adopted child, beloved and secure in His family forever.

Confessional Tie-in:

“God of his infinite mercy gave us his only begotten Son, who took upon him the form of a servant, and became like unto men, really assuming the true human nature, with all its weaknesses, sin excepted.” — Belgic Confession, Article 19

Prayer Prompt:

Father, thank You for sending Christ at just the right time to redeem me and make me Your child. Help me trust Your perfect timing in my circumstances today and live as one who truly belongs to Your family.

Go Deeper:

Consider how God’s timing in your own life has proven perfect, even when it didn’t feel that way. Meditate on what it means to be God’s adopted child—what privileges and responsibilities does this relationship bring to your daily interactions and decisions?

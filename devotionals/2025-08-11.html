In the way of your testimonies I delight as much as in all riches.
Psalm 119:14 (ESV)

Finding True Treasure in God’s Word

Psalm 119:14 (ESV) “In the way of your testimonies I delight as much as in all riches.”

Reflection

What does this text teach us? The psalmist makes a striking comparison that reveals where his heart truly finds satisfaction. He declares that walking in God’s testimonies—His revealed truth and commands—brings him the same joy that others find in accumulating wealth. This isn’t merely saying God’s Word is valuable; it’s saying that obedience to Scripture produces delight equal to the greatest earthly treasures.

The Hebrew word for “delight” suggests intense pleasure and satisfaction, the kind of joy that bubbles up from deep within. The psalmist has discovered something profound: God’s testimonies don’t merely contain information about how to live—they contain the very pathway to joy itself. His testimonies are His witness about Himself, His character, His will, and His ways with His people.

This speaks to a fundamental truth about human nature and desire. We were created to find our ultimate satisfaction in God Himself, and His Word is one of the primary means by which we encounter Him. While the world chases after riches, status, and temporal pleasures, the believer who has tasted the goodness of God’s truth knows where real treasure lies.

But notice the psalmist doesn’t just say he delights in reading God’s testimonies—he delights “in the way of” them. This refers to actually walking in obedience, living according to God’s revealed will. The joy comes not from mere intellectual knowledge but from the lived experience of following God’s design for human flourishing.

This challenges our natural tendency to compartmentalize faith from daily living. Do we truly find more delight in meditating on Scripture and living according to its principles than in pursuing worldly success? When we’re honest, we often find our hearts drawn more powerfully toward earthly treasures than heavenly ones.

Yet this verse also offers hope. The psalmist’s delight wasn’t manufactured through willpower—it flowed from experiencing God’s goodness through His Word. As we consistently expose ourselves to Scripture and seek to walk in its truth, the Spirit works to align our affections with God’s heart. The more we taste His goodness through His testimonies, the more our delight grows, and earthly riches lose their luster in comparison to the inexhaustible riches found in knowing and obeying our Creator.

Confessional Tie-in

“The whole counsel of God, concerning all things necessary for his own glory, man’s salvation, faith, and life, is either expressly set down in scripture, or by good and necessary consequence may be deduced from scripture.” — Westminster Confession of Faith, Chapter 1, Article 6

Prayer Prompt

Father, forgive me for the times my heart has chased after temporary treasures while neglecting the lasting riches found in Your Word. Transform my affections so that I truly find my greatest delight in knowing and obeying You through Your testimonies.

Go Deeper

This week, before opening your Bible, ask God to give you a heart that delights in His truth more than worldly gain. Consider: What “riches” in your life currently compete with Scripture for your attention and affection? How might walking more faithfully in God’s testimonies bring deeper joy than pursuing those temporal treasures?

Train up a child in the way he should go; even when he is old he will not depart from it.
Proverbs 22:6 (ESV)

The Foundation That Lasts

Proverbs 22:6 (ESV) “Train up a child in the way he should go; even when he is old he will not depart from it.”

Reflection

What does this text teach us? Solomon presents a profound truth about the lasting impact of godly instruction. The Hebrew word for “train” carries the idea of dedicating or initiating, suggesting intentional, purposeful guidance rather than casual influence. “The way he should go” literally means “according to his way” – pointing to training that considers both God’s standards and the unique design of each child.

This proverb reveals God’s ordinary means of transmitting faith across generations. While we cannot guarantee salvation through human effort – for regeneration is God’s sovereign work – He typically uses faithful parents and communities as instruments of grace. The promise that a child “will not depart from it” speaks to the deep, lasting impression of consistent, loving instruction rooted in God’s truth.

Yet this passage humbles us. Parents cannot manufacture faith in their children’s hearts, but they bear the weighty responsibility to faithfully plant and water seeds of truth. Every Bible story shared, every prayer offered, every moment of discipline done in love becomes part of God’s means of grace in a young life.

The principle extends beyond parenting to the entire Christian community. Churches, mentors, and mature believers participate in this sacred work of training. We recognize that spiritual formation happens through relationships, not merely through information transfer.

This verse also offers hope to those who received godly training but wandered. The deep foundations laid in childhood often become the path back home when prodigals remember truth learned at their parents’ knees. God’s faithfulness to His covenant promises spans generations.

For those who lacked such training, take heart – your heavenly Father provides what was missing. The church becomes your family, offering the nurturing guidance that shapes disciples. Whether we’re training others or being trained ourselves, we participate in God’s grand design for spiritual growth through community.

Confessional Tie-in

“The visible church… is the kingdom of the Lord Jesus Christ, the house and family of God, out of which there is no ordinary possibility of salvation.” — Westminster Confession of Faith, Chapter XXV, Article 2

Prayer Prompt

Father, thank You for using ordinary people to accomplish Your extraordinary work of training hearts for Your kingdom. Grant wisdom to all who guide the young, and help us faithfully participate in this sacred calling, trusting You for the growth that only You can give.

Go Deeper

Consider how God has used others to train you in His ways, and thank Him for their faithfulness. If you’re involved in training others – whether as parent, teacher, or mentor – ask yourself: am I pointing them toward Christ’s character and God’s truth? Reflect on how your local church serves as God’s family for both giving and receiving this formative training.

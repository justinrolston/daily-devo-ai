In the way of your testimonies I delight as much as in all riches.
Psalm 119:14 (ESV)

Delight in the Word: Riches That Never Fade

Reflection:

What does this text teach us? The psalmist makes a striking comparison: God’s testimonies—His revealed Word and commands—bring delight equal to “all riches.” This isn’t forced piety or religious duty speaking; it’s genuine joy. Psalm 119, the longest chapter in Scripture, repeatedly celebrates God’s law not as burden but as treasure.

Consider the cultural context: riches represented security, status, and the ability to shape one’s future. Yet the psalmist places Scripture on equal footing with everything wealth promises to provide. This reveals something profound about how a heart transformed by grace comes to value God’s Word.

This delight doesn’t come naturally to us. Left to ourselves, we love darkness rather than light, and God’s commands feel restrictive rather than delightful. But when the Spirit regenerates a heart, He gives new affections. What once seemed burdensome becomes beautiful. What once felt like restriction reveals itself as the path of life. This is the fruit of grace, not human effort—we cannot manufacture delight in God’s Word through sheer willpower.

Yet this delight also grows through disciplined attention to Scripture. Sanctification involves both gift and pursuit. As we read, meditate, and obey, the Spirit deepens our taste for what is good. The psalmist elsewhere confesses he was afflicted before he learned God’s statutes (v. 71)—suffering itself became a teacher, driving him deeper into the Word he now treasures.

For believers, this raises an honest question: does Scripture function as treasure in your life, or has it become mere obligation? Perhaps you’ve drifted into treating devotional time as a box to check rather than a feast to enjoy. Or perhaps genuine delight has grown cold under life’s pressures. Take heart—this is precisely where grace meets us. The same God who authored these testimonies also promises to renew our love for them.

Ultimately, all Scripture points to Christ, the living Word who fulfilled every testimony perfectly on our behalf. Our delight in God’s Word flows from our delight in the Savior it reveals.

Confessional Tie-in:

“The Holy Scripture is most necessary, those former ways of God’s revealing His will unto His people being now ceased… [it is] to be believed, and obeyed.” — Westminster Confession of Faith, Chapter 1, Article 1

Prayer Prompt:

Father, thank You for giving us Your Word as a treasure beyond earthly wealth. Forgive me for the times I’ve treated Scripture as duty rather than delight. Renew in me, by Your Spirit, a genuine love for Your testimonies that surpasses every earthly pleasure.

Go Deeper:

This week, before opening Scripture, pause and ask God to give you fresh delight in what you’re about to read—treating it as a treasure hunt rather than a task. Consider journaling one truth daily that particularly strikes you as valuable, training your heart to notice riches you might otherwise rush past. Ask yourself: what competing “riches” tend to steal my affection away from God’s Word?

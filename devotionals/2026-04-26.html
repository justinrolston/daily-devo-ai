Christ’s Perfect and Eternal Intercession

Hebrews 7:25 (ESV)
“Consequently, he is able to save to the uttermost those who draw near to God through him, since he always lives to make intercession for them.”

Reflection

What does this remarkable text teach us about our Savior’s ongoing work? The writer of Hebrews presents us with one of Scripture’s most comforting truths: Christ’s ability to save completely and His perpetual ministry of intercession.

The phrase “save to the uttermost” speaks to both the extent and completeness of Christ’s salvation. He saves completely—not partially, not temporarily, but fully and finally. This salvation reaches the worst of sinners and extends to the end of time. No one who comes to God through Christ is beyond the reach of His saving power, and no circumstance can diminish His ability to deliver.

Notice the pathway: salvation comes to “those who draw near to God through him.” We cannot approach the holy God directly in our sinful state. Christ alone is the mediator, the bridge between heaven and earth. Through His perfect life, atoning death, and victorious resurrection, He has opened the way for sinners to approach God with confidence.

But Christ’s work didn’t end at the cross or even at the resurrection. The text reveals His ongoing ministry: “he always lives to make intercession for them.” Right now, at this very moment, our risen Savior stands before the Father pleading our case. When we stumble in our walk, when we fail in our obedience, when Satan accuses us, Christ intercedes. He presents His own righteousness on our behalf and pleads His finished work as the ground for our acceptance.

This truth transforms how we face each day. In our weakness, we have a strong advocate. In our guilt, we have a perfect priest. In our uncertainty, we have an unchanging intercessor. Christ’s intercession isn’t occasional or conditional—it’s constant and comprehensive. He understands our struggles because He was tempted in every way, yet without sin.

How should this affect our daily living? It should drive us to bold prayer, confident that our High Priest welcomes us into God’s presence. It should comfort us in our failures, knowing that Christ’s advocacy never ceases. And it should humble us, remembering that our access to God depends entirely on Christ’s ongoing work, not our own merit or performance.

Confessional Tie-in

“Christ, in the work of mediation, acts according to both natures, by each nature doing that which is proper to itself; yet, by reason of the unity of the person, that which is proper to one nature is sometimes in Scripture attributed to the person denominated by the other nature.” — Westminster Confession of Faith 8.7

Prayer Prompt

Lord Jesus, thank You that Your saving work continues even now as You intercede for me before the Father. Help me rest in the completeness of Your salvation and approach God’s throne with confidence, knowing that my acceptance depends not on my performance but on Your perfect advocacy.

Go Deeper

Consider specific areas where you need Christ’s intercession today—perhaps a recurring struggle with sin, a difficult relationship, or an uncertain future. Remember that His intercession covers every aspect of your life. Meditate on Romans 8:34 and 1 John 2:1-2 to deepen your understanding of Christ’s ongoing priestly ministry.

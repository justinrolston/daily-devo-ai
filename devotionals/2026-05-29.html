But the steadfast love of the LORD is from everlasting to everlasting on those who fear him, and his righteousness to children's children, to those who keep his covenant and remember to do his commandments.
Psalm 103:17-18 (ESV)

God’s Unshakeable Love Across Generations

Reflection

What does this text teach us about the character of God and His relationship with His people? Psalm 103:17-18 presents us with one of Scripture’s most magnificent declarations about the steadfast love of the Lord. The Hebrew word for “steadfast love” is hesed, which encompasses God’s loyal, covenant love—a love that is both faithful and enduring.

Notice the scope of this love: it is “from everlasting to everlasting.” This isn’t merely a long time; it transcends time altogether. Before creation began, God’s love existed, and when all earthly things pass away, His love will remain unchanged. This everlasting quality of God’s love flows from His very nature—He is love, and therefore His love cannot diminish or fail.

But this passage also reveals the recipients of this steadfast love: “those who fear him.” Biblical fear of the Lord isn’t terror, but reverent awe that recognizes God’s holiness and responds with humble submission. This fear manifests itself in practical obedience—keeping His covenant and remembering to do His commandments. The text doesn’t suggest we earn God’s love through obedience; rather, genuine fear of the Lord naturally produces faithful living.

The generational aspect here is profound. God’s righteousness extends “to children’s children,” showing how covenant faithfulness ripples through families and communities. When we walk in covenant relationship with God, our faithfulness becomes a blessing that extends far beyond our own lives.

This should both humble and encourage us. We’re reminded that our spiritual legacy matters deeply. The choices we make today in following Christ affect not only our own relationship with God but potentially influence generations to come. At the same time, we can rest in the security of God’s unchanging character. His love for His people isn’t based on our performance but flows from His eternal nature.

In our rapidly changing world, where relationships often prove fragile and commitments temporary, we anchor our souls in the unshakeable reality of God’s everlasting love. This love sustains us through seasons of doubt, carries us through times of failure, and motivates us toward lives of grateful obedience.

Confessional Tie-in

“The love of God to the elect is unchangeable. He never doth, nor ever will, love them less than He doth. There is no inconstancy nor shadow of turning in God; and therefore His love cannot be changeable.” — Westminster Confession of Faith, Chapter 8, Section 8

Prayer Prompt

Lord, I marvel at Your steadfast love that reaches from everlasting to everlasting. Help me to live in reverent fear of You, not from terror but from awe at Your holiness. Grant me grace to keep Your covenant faithfully, knowing that my obedience flows from Your unchanging love, not as a means to earn it.

Go Deeper

Consider how your current choices in faithfulness might impact the spiritual legacy you leave for future generations. Meditate on specific ways God’s steadfast love has sustained you through difficult seasons, and let that fuel your gratitude and commitment to walk in His ways today.
